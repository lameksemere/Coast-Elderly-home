Our Mission & Story

Our Story

It started with one grandmother, forgotten

In 2017, our founder noticed an elderly neighbor hadn't left her house in weeks — no visitors, no help with meals, nothing. That single encounter sparked Golden Years Care: a simple promise that no senior in our community should be forgotten.

Nine years later, we've grown into a network of trained caregivers, volunteers and donors supporting over a thousand seniors with home visits, meals, healthcare access and genuine companionship — all rooted in dignity and transparency.
