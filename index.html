Why This Matters

Aging shouldn't mean being alone

Millions of seniors face isolation, mobility struggles, and limited access to nutritious food or medical attention. Small, consistent support changes everything for them.

🏠

Isolation at home

Many elderly live alone with family far away, going days without meaningful human contact.

🍲

Food insecurity

Fixed incomes and mobility limits make regular, nutritious meals harder to access.

🩺

Limited healthcare access

Transport and cost barriers mean routine checkups and medication often get missed.

🚶

Mobility & safety risks

Falls and injuries at home are common, and often go unnoticed for hours or days.

What Your Support Funds

Why donate to Coast Elderly Home

Every contribution is converted directly into real, trackable care — not overhead. Here's exactly what your donation makes possible.

🤝

Companionship visits

$18 funds a full home visit — conversation, safety checks, and genuine human connection for someone who may see no one else that week.

🥗

Daily hot meals

$8 provides one nutritionally balanced hot meal delivered directly to a senior's door by our care team.

💊

Medical support

$45 covers a home health check, medication delivery, or transport to a routine clinic appointment.
